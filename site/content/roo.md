One finger hole makes the second note. With the hole closed the whistle sounds the low note, and with the hole open it sounds the high note.

## The hole

The hole is on top of the back, on the centre line, in a shallow dimple. Either hand finds it by feel. The hole is 7.05 mm across in the model and 7.25 mm in the print file, because printed holes come out small. A fingertip must seal it, and the limit is 8 mm (C8).

The limit is a judgment. The acoustics notes found no measurements of fingertips. They judge that a child needs a hole of 8 mm or less.

The bore is straight for 2.5 mm and then opens downward under the roof. A plain bore lost: it needs a wider hole for the same note.

A change of 0.1 mm in the diameter moves the open note by 7.2 cents. The closed note does not move. A printed hole can be reamed wider, and wax on its wall makes it smaller.

## What this board does not show

The roo is a glide. A fingertip rocks over the hole, and the note rolls up and back. No model here follows a moving finger or the jet under it. A person must do it on a built part and listen (C25).
